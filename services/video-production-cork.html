Video production and videography in Cork

A Cork videographer and video production team for brand films, documentaries, commercials and social cuts across Ireland.

Most businesses looking for a videographer in Cork are stuck between two bad options: a one-person operator who can shoot but not plan, or a Dublin production company that charges travel and books out six weeks ahead. Neither buys you the thing you actually need: a film that fits your brand, delivered on your timeline, with the social cuts your channels run on.

HYPE is a Cork video company. We plan, shoot and edit for hospitality, retail, property and food and drink brands across Ireland. Brand films, documentaries, commercials and the short-form cuts for Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn, all out of one in-house team.

Who it's for

  • Brands launching something new, from a collection to a venue to a development.
  • Businesses with a story a photo can't tell: a founder, a chef, a craft, a cause.
  • Hospitality operators who need the room on film, not just in posts.
  • Property developers and agents who need listing films and documentary-style project work.

What's included

  • Pre-production. A story call, a treatment, a shot list and a shoot schedule, approved by you before anything is filmed.
  • The shoot. Usually two days on location, with a crew sized to the project.
  • Length to fit. From a 60-second brand film to a 30-minute documentary, like the Castle Freke Gin brand documentary.
  • Social cuts. 10 to 20 short versions for Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn, depending on the package.
  • Two rounds of revisions on the edit.
  • Portal and ad-ready delivery in the formats your channels actually use.

How it works

  1. Story call. We find the one thing the film has to make people feel.
  2. Treatment. One-page plan: the story, the people, the locations, the look.
  3. Shoot. Usually two days, with a crew sized to the project. We keep it small so your team acts like themselves.
  4. Edit and delivery. About three weeks from the shoot for short films; about six weeks for documentaries over 15 minutes.

Video production FAQ

What's the difference between a brand film and a commercial?

A brand film is a two to three minute story that tells people who you are. A commercial is a 15 to 60 second ad that asks for a specific action. We cut both from one shoot where it makes sense.

How long does it take?

About three weeks from the shoot for short films, about six weeks for documentaries over 15 minutes.

Do you film outside Cork?

Yes, across Ireland and abroad. Travel outside County Cork is charged separately.

Do you make documentaries?

Yes. Up to 30 minutes, like the Castle Freke Gin brand documentary. Quoted to scope.

Can you shoot and run social media too?

Yes. Film and social media management run from one HYPE team. Most brand film clients add social once the film is delivered, so the film keeps earning its keep.
