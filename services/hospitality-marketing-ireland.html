Hospitality marketing in Ireland that fills seats

Full-stack marketing for Irish hotels, restaurants and bars: film, social and PR, under one roof.

Most hospitality brands know their marketing should be better. The usual block is time, not budget: the week is for running the room, not briefing an agency. The second block is fragmentation. A photographer. A social person. A PR agency. Three invoices, three personalities, no single picture.

HYPE runs the full hospitality content system from Cork. One team shoots your brand films, runs your social media, and handles PR and brand positioning. One brief, one creative direction, one monthly report. You spend your time on the floor, not in agency meetings.

How it works

  1. Kick-off. We learn the room, the regulars and what a good week looks like for you.
  2. Film the brand. We shoot the hero brand film in your first month, so every social post that follows has a world to live in.
  3. Monthly rhythm. Shoot → plan → approve → post → report. Same dates every month.
  4. Review every six months. Numbers, bookings and the stories that landed, in one conversation. Then we plan the next six.

Hospitality marketing FAQ

Do I need both brand films and social media management?

Not on day one, but most of our hospitality clients end up on both inside the first six months. The brand film gives the social content a world to live in. The social keeps the brand film alive.

Do you work with venues outside Cork?

Yes, across Ireland. Social retainers that need monthly on-location shoots work best inside County Cork; film projects run anywhere, with travel outside the county charged separately.

What size of venue do you work with?

Independent restaurants, bars and hotels through to multi-site groups. The approach stays the same; the shoot cadence and campaign scope scale with the room count.

How quickly can you start?

Most retainers can start inside two weeks of signing. Brand film shoots are scheduled around your calendar and the room's quietest days.

Can you handle a launch or reopening?

Yes. Launches are quoted as a separate project, with a brand film, a social campaign and a press push built around the opening date.
