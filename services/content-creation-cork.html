Content creation FAQ

Why not just hire a content creator in-house?

Hiring in-house means a full-time salary, holidays, equipment and the risk of the wrong hire. HYPE delivers the same monthly output of shoots and edits for €2,700/month, with a whole team behind it instead of one person.

Do I get the raw footage?

Yes. Every shoot delivers finished edits for publication plus the raw footage, graded stills and project files to your drive so you own the library forever.

Which platforms do you create for?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X and LinkedIn, plus Bluesky if your customers are there. Reels, carousels, stories, long-form posts, event coverage. We cut each piece to the platform rather than reposting the same clip five ways.

Can you cover events as well as the monthly shoot?

Yes. Event coverage is included in the monthly package: launches, openings, special nights, any day the room matters more than usual.

Do I need to brief every shoot?

No. Once we've learned the brand in the first month, every subsequent shoot is planned by HYPE against your calendar. You approve the plan. We shoot and deliver.

Is this different from social media management?

Related but not identical. Social media management covers content creation plus posting, community management and ads. Content creation on its own is the shoots and edits, if you handle posting yourself.
