Social Media

Social media management in Cork that runs without you

On-location shoots, posting, community management and ads. You approve. We do the rest.

Most Cork business owners know their social media matters. They also know it's the first thing to slip when the week gets busy. Posts go out late, then not at all. The content looks different every month. Messages sit unanswered.

HYPE is the social media marketing agency and social media manager Cork businesses hire when the DIY and the freelance hire both stop working. We shoot real content at your venue every month, plan it, post it, answer your community and run your ads. You approve. We do the rest.

Who it's for

  • Restaurants, pubs and bars who need the room to look full online before it's full on a Friday.
  • Leisure and wellness businesses that sell an experience people have to see to want.
  • Retail and food and drink brands that need content at the speed of a launch.
  • Estate agents and property businesses whose listings need to stand out in a feed full of the same photos.

We run social media for venues including The Shelbourne Bar, Franciscan Well, The Raven Bar, Da Mirco Osteria and The Hive Padel Club.

What's included

  • A monthly content shoot at your venue, filmed by our own team, plus extra coverage of your events. Real people, real place, not stock.
  • A monthly content plan you see and approve before anything goes live.
  • Posting on every platform your customers use: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X and LinkedIn, and Bluesky if your customers are there.
  • Reels, carousels and stories edited in-house.
  • Community management: comments and messages handled, with anything that needs you passed to your team.
  • Paid social ads: we set up, monitor and report on your Meta and TikTok campaigns, with creative cut from the same monthly on-location shoots. The ad budget is paid separately, directly to the platforms.

How it works

  1. Kick-off. We learn the business, the customers and what a good month looks like for you.
  2. First shoot. We build a bank of content in one session at your venue.
  3. Monthly rhythm. Shoot → plan → approve → post. Same dates every month.
  4. Six-month partnership. Contracts run for six months. We review the results together before the next six.

The 24-hour rule

Every client message is answered within 24 hours, and anything urgent goes straight to the right person on your team. Slow replies and late posts are the two most common reasons businesses leave their last agency. We built HYPE around fixing both.

Pricing

Social media management is €2,700 per month + VAT, including setting up, monitoring and reporting on your ads, on a six-month contract. The ad budget is separate and paid directly to the platforms. Websites and SEO are quoted separately. You get a fixed monthly price before you sign.

Social media management FAQ

How long is the contract?

Six months. The first month sets up the content and the rhythm, and the results build after that. Six months is enough time to judge it properly.

Do I need to create any content myself?

No. We shoot it every month and cover your events. If you want to send us photos or clips from the week, we'll use them, but you don't have to.

Which platforms do you manage?

All of them: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X and LinkedIn, plus Bluesky if your customers are there. We focus your budget on the two or three where your customers actually spend time.

Are ads included?

Setting up, monitoring and reporting on your ads is included in the monthly price. The ad budget itself is not: it's paid directly to Meta or TikTok, so you see every euro and decide how much to spend.

Do you only work with Cork businesses?

Most of our social media clients are in County Cork, because we shoot on location every month.
