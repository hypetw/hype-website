Corporate Video

Corporate video production in Cork

A Cork video company for recruitment films, company culture videos, event coverage and the long-form corporate work most agencies turn down. From €3,300 + VAT.

Corporate video is where most video companies in Cork cut corners. A one-day shoot. A talking head. A talking head's HR director with a logo over the shoulder. Nobody watches it. Nobody shares it. The role still does not get filled.

HYPE makes corporate video that behaves like brand film, because the brief is the same: show somebody who was not there what the place is actually like. Recruitment campaigns, company culture pieces, event films, product explainers, built by a Cork team that cuts the social edits as well as the hero.

Who it's for

  • Businesses hiring at scale who need a recruitment film that attracts the people they actually want.
  • Growing companies whose culture is their competitive edge and needs to be visible from outside.
  • Event organisers who need the day captured as a film, not a photo album.
  • Product and service businesses that have to explain what they do before the sale happens.

What's included

  • Pre-production. A story call, a written treatment, a shot list and a shoot schedule. Approved before anyone turns up with a camera.
  • The shoot. Usually two days on location with a crew sized to the project. Bigger multi-location productions are planned to scope.
  • The film. Hero film from 60 seconds to 10 minutes, longer corporate documentaries up to 30 minutes.
  • Ten to twenty social cuts for Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn, so the hero film does not die on your website.
  • Two rounds of revisions on the edit, included in the fixed price.
  • Delivery in the exact formats your channels, HR portal and ad platforms need.

How it works

  1. Story call. We agree the audience and the single thing the film has to communicate.
  2. Treatment. One-page plan: story, people, locations, look.
  3. Shoot. Usually two days. A small crew so your team acts like themselves on camera.
  4. Edit and delivery. About three weeks from the shoot for short films, about six for anything over 15 minutes.

Corporate video FAQ

What counts as corporate video?

Recruitment films, company culture videos, product explainers, training content, event coverage, internal communications, investor and partner films. Anything a brand uses to explain itself to employees, buyers or stakeholders.

How long does it take?

About three weeks from the shoot to final delivery for short films. Documentary-scale corporate films over 15 minutes take about six weeks. We agree the delivery date before the shoot.

How many social cuts do I get?

Ten to twenty short-form versions for Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn, depending on the package. The hero film is for your website and recruitment pages; the cuts are what feeds your channels for months after.

Do you film outside Cork?

Yes, across Ireland. Travel outside County Cork is charged separately. Multi-location corporate shoots are planned to scope.

How many rounds of revisions?

Two rounds of changes on the edit, included in the fixed price. We keep the brief tight at the treatment stage so revisions are refinements, not rebuilds.

Can you handle recruitment campaigns across multiple roles?

Yes. One shoot can produce the hero recruitment film plus role-specific cuts, with the social edits that make the campaign land on LinkedIn and TikTok.
