A brand film is the two minutes that tell a customer who you are before they ever walk in.

Most businesses in Cork have hundreds of posts and not one piece of film that explains why they exist. That's the gap we fill.

Who brand films are for

Brand films work best when something has to be explained, not just shown:

  • A launch. A new collection, a new venue, a new development. You need people to care before day one.
  • A story with a person behind it. A founder, a chef, a distiller, a local legend. People buy from people.
  • A premium price. If you charge more than the place down the road, the film has to show why.
  • A brand that looks like its competitors online. Film is the fastest way to stop looking like everyone else in the feed.

What's included

  • Pre-production. A story call, a written treatment, a shot list and a shoot schedule. You approve the plan before a camera comes out.
  • The shoot. Usually two days on location with a crew sized to the project, in Cork or anywhere in Ireland. Bigger projects are planned to scope.
  • The film. From a 60-second brand film to a 30-minute documentary, edited and finished in-house.
  • Social cuts. 10 to 20 short versions for Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn, depending on the package.
  • Revisions. Two rounds of changes.
  • Delivery. Files ready for your website, social channels and ads.

How it works

  1. Story call. We find the one thing the film has to make people feel.
  2. Treatment. A one-page plan: the story, the people, the locations, the look.
  3. Shoot. Usually two days. We keep the crew small so your team and customers act like themselves.
  4. Edit and delivery. Short films arrive about three weeks after the shoot. Documentaries over 15 minutes take about six weeks.

Brand film FAQ

How long should a brand film be?

Most brand films run between 60 seconds and 3 minutes: long enough to tell one story properly, short enough that people finish it. Documentaries can run up to 30 minutes. The social cuts reach people; the hero film convinces them.

How long does it take?

About three weeks from the shoot to delivery for short films, and about six weeks for documentaries over 15 minutes.

Do you film outside Cork?

Yes, across Ireland and abroad. Travel outside County Cork is charged separately.

What's the difference between a brand film and an ad?

An ad asks for something now. A brand film gives people a reason to remember you, so your ads work better later. We cut ads from the brand film footage, so you get both from one shoot.

Can you make a documentary?

Yes. A brand documentary is a longer, observational film built around real people, like our film for Castle Freke Gin. It's quoted to scope.

Do I need a script?

No. We write the treatment with you. For documentary-style films we interview people rather than have them read lines.
