Property Content

Property content and listing imagery for Ireland

Editorial listing imagery, films and sales collateral for Irish estate agents and developers.

Property marketing in Ireland has a visibility problem. Portals look the same. Most listings use the same four interior shots. New developments launch with CAD renders that read like spec sheets. The listings that sell faster are not the ones with more features. They are the ones a buyer can see themselves in.

HYPE produces property content for estate agents and developers across Ireland. Editorial listing imagery, launch films, brochures and sales collateral. For developers running a full launch, this feeds into HYPE Listing Studio, our dedicated offer for scheme-wide visual programmes.

Talk to us about your scheme →

Who it's for

  • Property developers launching new residential or commercial schemes.
  • Estate agencies whose premium listings need visuals that match the price.
  • Architecture and design firms who want their built work documented properly.
  • Landlords and operators of serviced properties where repeat demand depends on how the room shows up online.

What's included

  • Editorial listing imagery. Shots framed and graded for consistency across every listing in a portfolio or scheme.
  • Property films. Walkthroughs and brand films cut for portals and social, from short cuts to full launch films.
  • Off-plan visualisation. Finished-home visuals buyers can picture living in, not technical renders.
  • Launch and sales collateral. Print brochures, digital decks and campaign assets, consistent across every touchpoint.
  • Scheme-wide creative direction. One team, one look, across every unit and every asset.

How it works

  1. Brief. We start with the property and the buyer. One brief, one creative direction.
  2. Production. Shot, edited and graded by one in-house team, in Cork and anywhere in Ireland.
  3. Delivery. Ready-to-publish assets, in the formats your portals, brochures and campaigns use. Most engagements move from brief to delivery in five to fourteen days.

HYPE Listing Studio

For developers running multi-property launches or estate agencies with a premium listing tier, we run a dedicated offer: HYPE Listing Studio. One creative direction across every asset in the launch cycle — the film, the imagery, the brochures and the social cuts — delivered on one timeline.

Pricing

Single-listing projects are quoted as a fixed price per scheme from €3,300 + VAT. Multi-property launches and scheme-wide programmes are quoted to scope through HYPE Listing Studio, from €1,500 per month. You get a fixed price before we start.

Property content FAQ

Can you work from imagery we already have?

Yes. Most of our listing-grade work is produced from existing imagery your photographer has already shot. For launch films and documentary pieces, we shoot on site.

What's the difference between this and HYPE Listing Studio?

This page is the entry point for property marketing work across Ireland: single-listing jobs, campaigns, agency content. HYPE Listing Studio is our dedicated offer for developers running scheme-wide launches, with a monthly engagement and one creative direction across every asset in the launch cycle.

Can you produce visuals for off-plan units?

Yes. We produce finished-home visuals buyers can picture living in, not technical architectural renders. Consistent look across every unit type in a scheme, delivered on one launch timeline.

Do you work with agencies as well as developers?

Yes. Premium estate agencies and property brands use the same production team, usually on a per-listing or per-scheme basis.

Do you work outside Ireland?

Yes. HYPE Listing Studio is available across Ireland, the UK and internationally; our flagship spec film is a Lyford Cay (Bahamas) beachfront estate.
