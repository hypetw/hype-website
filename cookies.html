Cookie policy

Cookies on hypedigital.ie.

We only run one tool, we tell you exactly what it does, and you choose. This page explains it in plain English.

Last updated 5 November 2026 HYPE Marketing & Media Ltd., Cork
01 — The short version

One tool. Analytics only. Your choice.

The only third-party tool on this site is Google Analytics 4. It tells us which pages are read and roughly where visitors come from. We do not run ad trackers, build visitor profiles, or share your data with third parties for marketing. The banner at the bottom of the page is how you tell us whether that's OK.

02 — What gets set, exactly

The three things that may live in your browser.

Nothing is set until you choose. If you click Accept, Google Analytics drops two cookies. We also store one small key in your browser's local storage so the banner doesn't come back on every page.

Name What it does Set by Lifetime
_ga A random visitor identifier so we can tell a new session from a returning one. Google Analytics 2 years
_ga_FT9LPT3CB9 Session state for our specific property. Google Analytics 2 years
hype_consent_v1 Remembers your Accept or Reject choice so the banner doesn't nag you on every page. Not a cookie — stored locally in your browser only, never shared. This site Until you clear it
03 — If you click Reject

Nothing happens, which is the point.

No cookies are set. No analytics scripts load. No pings to Google. We remember you declined so we don't ask again this session.

Rejecting doesn't break anything on the site. You get exactly the same pages, the same films, the same forms. We just see less about how you found us.

04 — Change your mind

Flip the choice any time.

Scroll to the bottom of any page and click Cookie settings. The banner comes back and you can accept or reject again. You can also clear your browser's cookies and local storage; the banner will reappear on your next visit.

05 — What Google does with the data

Aggregated usage, with IP anonymisation on.

Google Analytics reports back to us as aggregated website usage: pages viewed, time on site, country, approximate city, device type. We have IP anonymisation enabled, so Google truncates visitor IPs before storage. The default data retention is set to 14 months, after which Google deletes event-level data from our property.

Google's own privacy policy covers everything they do on their side: policies.google.com/privacy.

06 — Your rights under GDPR

Ask us anything.

Under GDPR you can ask us what data we hold on you, correct it, or have it deleted. Because GA4 does not collect anything that identifies you personally — no name, no email, no login — there is rarely anything meaningful to request. But if you want to confirm, write to hello@hypedigital.ie and we will respond within 24 hours.

If you believe we are mishandling your data, you can raise a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission: dataprotection.ie.
